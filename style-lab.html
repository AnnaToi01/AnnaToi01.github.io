typographyFont shortlist

All free on Google Fonts, so no files to host. Funky ones up top, workhorses at the bottom. The strongest move on nearly every site I looked at was one characterful display face + one very plain body face — not three funky fonts at once.

Instrument Serif — display
Microbes & evolution
Elegant, high contrast, free italic. What pearlitegates uses. Handles italic species names natively.
